---
title: Future research
description: Research directions for the next five years.
---

My near-term plan has three connected directions.

1. **GeoAI that holds up in new places.** Mapping models often lose accuracy when they are moved to a new region, season, or sensor. I am developing multi-sensor fusion models that report their own uncertainty and are evaluated with spatially explicit validation. The first test case is a NASA NISAR project selected in 2026, where I develop the algorithms that identify inundation in forested wetlands from NISAR, SWOT, and Sentinel-1.
2. **Chronic coastal flooding and who is exposed to it.** Sea level rise is turning occasional floods into frequent ones. I combine satellite time series with topography, census data, and social vulnerability indices to identify communities in North Carolina and the Southeast that face both climate and social stress.
3. **Urban analytics for heat, flooding, and health.** I am adapting canopy-height and structure mapping to cities, to map tree canopy, building height, and impervious surfaces and relate them to heat exposure and health.
